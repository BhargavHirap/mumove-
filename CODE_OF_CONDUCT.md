# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MUMOVE aims to be a welcoming, respectful and constructive space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or viewpoint.

We want participation to be free of harassment and intimidation. Be kind, assume good intent where reasonable, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep reports and technical discussions factual and useful.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or unwanted advances.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Trolling, sustained disruption or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

To report a conduct concern, contact the project maintainer privately through the GitHub profile: https://github.com/BhargavHirap. Please do not turn a sensitive report into a public issue.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions.
